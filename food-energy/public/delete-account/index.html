Account & Data Deletion

Food Energy (developer: Sergey Mochalin) · Last updated: June 11, 2026

Food Energy lets you optionally sign in (with Apple or Google) to back up your food diary to the cloud. You can delete your data, or your entire account, at any time — both options are below.

Delete your data (keep your account)

  1. Open Food Energy and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Open Privacy & Data.
  3. Tap “Delete All Data” and confirm.

This permanently deletes all your meals and meal photos — both on the device and in the cloud backup. Your account stays signed in.

Delete your account and all data

  1. Open Food Energy and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Settings, tap “Cloud sync” to open the Account screen.
  3. Tap “Delete account & data” and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all cloud-stored data.

Delete by request

If you can no longer access the app, email sergmochalin@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Food Energy account or data”. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity link (Apple / Google)
  • Your cloud-backed food diary — meals, calories and macros, dates
  • Your daily goal and body metrics
  • Meal photos you uploaded

Deletion is permanent and applies immediately (in-app) or within 30 days (by email request).

What is retained

  • Nothing linked to your identity is retained after deletion.
  • Anonymous, abuse-prevention request counters (not linked to you) are auto-deleted within 48 hours.
  • Data stored only on your device is not affected by account deletion — remove it by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Cloud backup is optional and exists only if you signed in. It is not available in the RuStore version of the app. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
